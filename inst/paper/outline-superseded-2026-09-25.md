> **Why this file exists.** The grant pipeline works because `/grant-outline` produces a
> checked-in outline that every `/draft-*` skill reads, so sections do not each reinvent the
> paper's structure or re-argue the same point. Papers had no equivalent. This is that file.

> "which section owns which idea", so that a claim is made once, in one place, at full strength.

**Grain rule for "Owns."** An item in this column must be traceable to something checkable
elsewhere — a claim in `claims.md` table 1, a result in `claims.md` table 2, or an
assumption/symbol in `notation.md` — or explicitly marked as owning nothing of its own for
connective material. **Specificity test applied above:** e.g. "identification of the ATE under
Assumption 2's overlap condition" — this outline avoids the generic "methodology" failure mode.

**Descriptive baselines, for calibration only.** Median across 206 papers: 9 intro paragraphs,
10 top-level sections, 2 intro "we `<verb>`" constructions, 3 theorem-family environments, 0
run-in pseudo-headers. This manuscript has **10 numbered theorem-family environments in the
body/appendix combined** (2 lemmas, 3 propositions, 2 corollaries, plus ~13 assumptions) — well
above the median 3, consistent with a methods-theory paper carrying its own proofs in an
appendix rather than citing them elsewhere. Not a finding; noted for calibration only.

## 3. Dependency and ordering check

- [x] **No section depends on a result introduced later.** Checked: Proposition 3 (§4.7) uses
      Assumptions `assump:cond-ident`, `assump:struct-stab`, `assump:target-dist`, all stated
      earlier in the same subsection. The asymptotic-normality proposition (§5) cites
      `Assumptions~\ref{RC1}--\ref{RC10}`, all of which are declared in Appendix B — this is a
      **body-cites-appendix** forward dependency, flagged below.
- [ ] **Every assumption is introduced before the first result that uses it — FLAG.** RC1-RC10
      are declared in `main.tex:804-937` (Appendix B, `\subsection{Regularity Conditions}`), but
      are cited by Proposition (Asymptotic distribution), stated in the *body* at `main.tex:617`
      (§5.1), before the appendix that defines them. This is the exact defect this checklist item
      exists to catch — a shared LaTeX counter numbers RC1-RC10 after the body result that
      invokes them by name. It does not break compilation (the citations are to labels, not to
      undefined numbers, since RC1-RC10 are all in the same document), but a reader following the
      citation `\ref{RC1}` on a first read of §5 will not yet have seen the condition.
      **Recorded here as a finding, not fixed** (no `.tex` edits in scope).
- [x] **Any assumption whose Declared-in field says `appendix` is not required by a body
      result — VIOLATED, see above.** RC1-RC10 are declared-in-appendix and required by a body
      proposition (§5's asymptotic-normality proposition). See `notation.md` §2a for the
      per-assumption record.
- [x] **Every range assumption citation has its current expansion recorded.** The manuscript's
      only range citations are `Assumptions~\ref{RC1}--\ref{RC10}` (§5, §App. B remark) and
      `Assumptions~\ref{RC8}--\ref{RC10}` (§4.7 EIF section, App. B remark). Both ranges are
      recorded in `notation.md` §2a with their current expansion (RC1 through RC10 inclusive;
      RC8 through RC10 inclusive). Neither range crosses a body/appendix boundary in its
      *endpoints* (both endpoints are in Appendix B), though the *citing* result is in the body
      for the first range — the same forward-dependency flag as above.
- [x] **The discussion contains at least one result from the substantive sections.** §7
      (Discussion) explicitly synthesizes Propositions 1-3 by name ("Path 1... the FATT is
      identified by the backward-looking ATT...", etc., `main.tex:732`) — passes.
- [x] **Appendix material is genuinely appendix material, and `\appendix` is used so numbering
      switches.** `main.tex:752` has `\appendix` immediately before Appendix A (`main.tex:754`).
      No pre-`\appendix` `\section{Appendix}` competes with it. Passes.

Recording these so a later reader does not mistake absence for oversight. `paper-protocol.md`
measured all of them and declined to prescribe:

  Author's choice; `main.tex`'s introduction does not carry one, which is within the observed

  `subsection` matches practice. `main.tex` has one, as `\subsection{Related work}` inside
  §1 — matches the majority placement among papers that have one.

  exceeds the 9.5%/35% measured norms, but naming is never a gate either way.
